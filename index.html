How do I get into the college

We currently have 3 modes of study at SQI College of ICT, the National Diploma, Professional Diploma Certificate, and Ordinary Professional Certificate Program. You can join us by applying through any of the modes of study.

How much is the tution?

This depends on the type of course you are applying for and the duration—whether it’s a National Diploma, Professional Diploma, or a short-term professional program.

Are your classes physical or virtual

Lectures at SQI College of ICT can either be physical, virtual or blended. You can decide to for anyone that meets your demand.

Will I get a job after my training?

78.5% of our students found secure employment within three months of graduation. Students graduate from class to land job roles immediately.
